## ✨ Overview

**Folkd** is a human-centric HR management suite designed to simplify corporate financial cycles and make payroll management more transparent, organized, and stress-free.

I developed the payroll module for Folkd, transforming complex compensation data into a clean, intuitive dashboard that helps HR teams monitor payroll performance, track payment statuses, review compensation breakdowns, and manage approval workflows with confidence.

Combining warm, organic tones with frosted-glass layering, the interface balances a welcoming visual identity with the precision required for financial operations.

---

## 💼 Why Teams (and Clients) Love This

- 💰 **Payroll KPI Cards** — Get a clear overview of key payroll metrics and financial performance at a glance
- 📈 **Cost Trend Charts** — Visualize payroll expenses and identify compensation trends over time
- 🧾 **Structured Compensation Breakdown** — Understand salaries, benefits, and taxes through organized data and breakdown meters
- 📋 **Dynamic Payslip Tables** — Manage employee payslips with dedicated Paid, Processing, and Pending status filters
- ✅ **Inline Approval Mechanics** — Review and approve payroll actions directly within the workflow
- 🗂️ **Integrated Task Checklists** — Keep payroll responsibilities organized and ensure important steps are not overlooked
- 🔄 **Streamlined Payroll Runs** — Review, calculate, and execute payroll processes through a structured interface
- 🔎 **Clear Financial Visibility** — Turn dense compensation information into digestible, easy-to-understand insights
- 🧊 **Frosted Glass UI** — Use layered surfaces and subtle transparency to create a modern dashboard experience
- 🎨 **Warm, Human-Centric Design** — Combine organic colors, clean layouts, and intuitive interactions for a more approachable HR platform
